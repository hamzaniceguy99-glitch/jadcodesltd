Program 02 · Keyboard and screen reader

Accessibility audits

Automated scanners catch a minority of real barriers. The ones that stop people using a site — a form you cannot complete with a keyboard, a modal that traps focus, an image carousel that announces nothing — are found by a person testing with the tools disabled users actually use. Our report lists failures against specific criteria, ranked by the impact on someone trying to complete a task.

  • 1 to 3 weeks
  • Organisations
  • Fixed fee
  • Access to the site

Program 03 · Fixes, then the statement

Remediation and statements

Public sector bodies must publish an accessibility statement that is accurate — one claiming full compliance on a site with known failures is worse than one listing them honestly with a plan. We fix what can be fixed, retest, and draft a statement that reflects reality, including the parts not yet resolved.

  • 2 to 8 weeks
  • Organisations
  • Fixed fee
  • Decisions on priorities

Program 04 · Or a clean exit

Handover and maintenance

Most sites regress because content editors add an unlabelled image or paste a heading that breaks the order. A short session on the handful of habits that matter does more for long-term accessibility than another audit, and it means you can leave us whenever you want.
